Diabetes details

Calories are split: Morning tiffin 25% · 10 AM snack 10% · Lunch 30% · 4 PM snack 10% · Dinner 25%. A vegetable salad is included in every meal.

✅ Advice

  • Do not skip meals; eat at fixed times.
  • Fill half the plate with vegetables / salad, a quarter with protein, a quarter with millet / roti.
  • Prefer jowar, ragi, bajra, korra (millets) and whole wheat; limit white rice.
  • Use little oil (about 3 teaspoons per person per day); steam, boil, grill or tawa-cook.
  • Drink 8–10 glasses of water. Walk 10–15 minutes after meals; exercise 30 minutes daily.
  • Check blood sugar as advised by the doctor.

⛔ Avoid

  • Sugar, jaggery, honey, sweets (kaja, pootharekulu, laddu, jilebi), cakes, biscuits.
  • Fruit juices, cold drinks, sweetened tea / coffee, tender coconut water in excess.
  • Banana, mango, sapota, grapes, jackfruit, dates.
  • Fried items (punugulu, bajji, vada, puri), bakery items, maida.
  • Potato, yam, sweet potato, arati (raw banana) in large amounts.
  • Large servings of white rice; skipping meals; alcohol.
